# Sequel Proposal — Skyborne: Skies End

## Overview

*Skyborne: Skies End* would be the sequel to my original game, *SkyBorne*. The sequel would expand the world, characters and lore established in the first game while introducing a much larger conflict.

The main villains of the sequel would be **Orsted Drake** and the **Gilded Gold**. Their absence from the main events of the first game is intentional, as their story is being saved for the sequel.

## Story

After the events of *SkyBorne*, the Exiled discovers that the events surrounding the dungeons and Great Runes were connected to something much larger.

Orsted Drake, the leader of the Gilded Gold, would become the main antagonist of the sequel. His true intentions and connection to the Great Runes would finally be revealed.

The Gilded Gold would become a major opposing faction, appearing throughout the world and interfering with the player's progression.

## New Features

- New areas and environments
- New NPCs
- Being able to create guilds yourself
- The Gilded Gold as a major enemy faction
- Orsted Drake as the main villain
- New enemies and bosses
- Improved combat mechanics
- New weapons and relics
- Expanded NPC dialogue
- More secret locations and lore
- More meaningful player choices
- Expanded faction and reputation systems

## Improvements

The sequel would improve on the original game's combat, UI, NPC interactions and overall code structure.

I would also use the sequel to introduce more advanced OOP concepts and create more complex systems than were possible in the first game.

## Goal

The goal of *Skyborne: Skies End* would be to expand upon the foundations of *SkyBorne* while answering the mysteries intentionally left unresolved.

The first game introduces the world.

**The sequel reveals what was hiding behind it.**
